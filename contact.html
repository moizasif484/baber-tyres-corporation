Our Story

Three Decades of Serving Karachi's Roads

Baber Tyres Corporation was established in 1990 on M.A. Jinnah Road, Karachi, with a simple goal: bring genuine, reliable tyres directly to local drivers without unnecessary middlemen.

Over 35 years, we've grown from a single dealership into a direct importer of leading international brands — while never losing the personal, straightforward service that built our name. Today, we serve everyone from everyday commuters to commercial fleet operators and industrial equipment owners.

Every tyre we sell is backed by professional fitting, computerized alignment and balancing, performed in-house by experienced technicians.
